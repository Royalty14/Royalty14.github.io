About Connections Jobs SA

Last updated 8 October 2026

Connections Jobs SA is a free website that collects open jobs, learnerships and internships across South Africa and puts them in one place, with closing dates you can see at a glance.

How it works

  • We check employer career sites, government department adverts and the DPSA vacancy circulars every day.
  • Every listing links straight to the official source: the employer's own careers page or the government circular. We don't send you to other job boards.
  • Expired listings are removed, and we never invent details. If something isn't stated in the advert (like a salary), we say so.

What we are not

We are not a recruitment agency and we don't take applications. You apply directly with the employer. We never charge job seekers anything, and no genuine employer will ask you to pay to apply.

Who runs it

Connections Jobs SA is an independent project run from Vryburg, North West, to make it easier for South Africans, especially young people, to find real opportunities. Follow us on Instagram at @connectionsjobssa for daily posts.
